How we teach

The system
behind the promise.

  • A working project every semester. Eight projects, each more demanding than the last.
  • A workshop called the Factory. Build in a lab set up like a company workshop, with evening access.
  • A personal kit for every student. Practice on your own equipment.
  • Individual practical tests. Build in a team and demonstrate the skills yourself.
  • AI as a tool. Use it to build faster and learn to check its work.
  • 16–20 weeks of engineering work. Spend your final semester in a company or the Product Studio.

Read all the answers
Will AI take my child’s job?

AI is changing every job. We teach students to build the chips, devices, machines and software AI depends on, and to use AI tools with judgment.

Is this a four-year degree?

The B.Tech programmes are designed as four-year degrees. Ask admissions for the awarding university, campus and approvals for the specific course.

Can my child start with no build experience?

Yes. The teaching plan starts with a beginners’ bootcamp, a personal kit and a second chance at each practical test.
